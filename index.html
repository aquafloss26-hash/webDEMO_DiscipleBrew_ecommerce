Our Story

The First Faith-Driven Café in the Philippines

Founded on December 8, 2023, Disciple Brew began with a simple vision: to create a space where coffee, community, and faith intersect. We believe in purpose over profit, connection over consumption, and meaningful moments over mere transactions.

Our name comes from the idea that we are all learners and followers of something greater. Just as coffee beans are pressed to release their flavor, we too are pressed by life's challenges — but never crushed. This resilience is at the heart of our community.
